XHTTP transport

Network → XHTTP transport adds a VLESS node over XHTTP (stream-one, HTTP/2, ALPN h2) next to every TLS VLESS node. Clients POST to https://<host>/xh (path configurable).

  • Xray-core clients: v2rayNG, v2rayN, Streisand, Hiddify on Xray
  • sing-box and Clash / Mihomo profiles skip it

Display options

Open Display in the panel's top bar. Saved in your browser only.

  • ThemeDark, light or match system.
  • AccentSignal lime, blue, violet, emerald, amber or rose.
  • TypefaceSF Pro (system), Google Sans (online) or bundled Inter.
  • Corners · density · textRound / soft / square, compact density, large text.
  • MotionFull or reduced.
  • BehaviourHide the ticker, blur your IP on load, confirm before sign-out.

Security & backup

  • PasswordPBKDF2-hashed in KV; login is rate-limited.
  • Kill switchStops every tunnel at once without touching users.
  • Backup / restoreExport settings and subscribers as JSON; import on any Worker.
