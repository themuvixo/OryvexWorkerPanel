The subscription link

Every subscriber has one link: https://<host>/sub/<token>. The Worker reads the app's user-agent and answers in the right format. Opened in a browser, it shows the subscriber's own status page with QR codes and one-tap import buttons.

Force a format

Add ?format= when an app guesses wrong.

Telegram Web Proxy

Turn it on in the panel and share the tg://webproxy link. Telegram Desktop and Android open it in one tap. Rotate the secret from the panel any time.

Private DoH

Each subscriber gets https://<host>/dns-query/<token>. Paste it into any DNS-over-HTTPS setting (browsers, Android Private DNS apps, sing-box).
